CONCEPT A — NOTES

  • Strongest "story" feel — the year rolling over is the narrative beat.
  • Scales to all 32 projects without layout changes (it's a sequence, not a grid).
  • Works on mobile as-is. Reduced-motion fallback is a simple list.
CONCEPT B

The horizontal ride

The page pins and your vertical scroll drives a horizontal journey. A ruler of year ticks slides past at a different speed than the cards (parallax), ghost year numerals drift beneath, and each project rises into place as it enters. Classic awwwards move.

CONCEPT B — NOTES

  • Most visually striking on desktop — the parallax ruler sells it.
  • 32 projects horizontally is a LONG ride; likely needs year-jump navigation.
  • On mobile it has to fall back to a vertical layout (two layouts to maintain).
CONCEPT C

Depth dive

Scrolling moves you through z-space — each project starts as a distant blur, flies toward you into focus, then sweeps past the camera as the next one approaches. A faint circle expands behind everything like you're travelling through it.
