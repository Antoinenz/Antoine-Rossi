Privacy Policy

Antoine Rossi · Last updated June 2026

This website is a personal portfolio. It is built to respect your privacy: it does not collect personal information, require an account, or track you.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and has no contact forms or logins. You can browse it without sharing any personal data with me.

Third-party services

A few services are involved in delivering the site, and they may process limited technical data as part of normal operation:

  • Cloudflare hosts and serves the site. As with any web host, it may process technical request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver content and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Font files are requested from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

External links

This site links to my other websites and projects, as well as third-party profiles (such as GitHub and LinkedIn). Once you follow a link, you are subject to that site's own privacy practices, not this one.

Your data

Because no personal data is collected or stored by this site, there is nothing for me to share, sell, or delete. I never sell data.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? You can reach me through the contact links on the homepage.
